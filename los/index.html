Feed Height = pedestal + half diameter (approx center of reflector). Sat elevation from Look Angles tool.

INPUT FIELDS EXPLAINED:
📐 Diameter — Reflector dish diameter in meters (e.g. 7.6m)
📍 Feed Height — Height from ground to the antenna feed center (typically pedestal + half diameter)
🏗️ Pedestal — Height of the pedestal/foundation above ground level
📡 Sat Elevation — Satellite elevation angle from Look Angles calculator
🧭 Sat Azimuth — Compass direction to the satellite (0°=North, 90°=East, 180°=South)

Obstructions

Distance = nearest point of building to antenna. Corner1 & Corner2 = compass bearings to two ends of the building face (for orientation). Leave blank to auto-face antenna.

OBSTRUCTION INPUT FIELDS:
📏 Distance — From antenna center to the NEAREST point of the obstruction (meters)
📐 Height — Top of the obstruction above ground level (meters)
🧭 Bearing — Compass direction from antenna to obstruction center (0°=N, 90°=E, 180°=S, 270°=W)
↔️ Width — How wide the building/obstruction face is (the side facing antenna, meters)
↕️ Depth — How deep the building goes away from antenna (perpendicular to width, meters)
🔸 Corner1° / Corner2° — Compass bearing to left & right edges of the building face (optional, for orientation)

HOW TO MEASURE IN THE FIELD:
1️⃣ Use a tape/laser to measure Distance from your antenna to the nearest wall
2️⃣ Estimate or measure the Height of the rooftop (count floors × 3.5m)
3️⃣ Use a compass to get the Bearing from your antenna toward the obstruction
4️⃣ For buildings: measure or estimate Width of the face you can see
5️⃣ Optional: take compass readings to both corners of the building for accurate orientation
